Find out if your crypto is reported to your tax office, and when

Since 2026, crypto platforms in a growing list of countries have had to hand your identity and your transaction history to a tax authority automatically, under the OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework. This page tells you whether that applies to you, which authority receives the data, and on what date.

Nothing you enter leaves this page. There is no server, no account, and no analytics on your answers. The page loads no third-party code and makes no network requests at all. You can turn off your connection and it will still work.

How this works

This tool matches the country you select against a small dataset of jurisdictions where a named legal instrument has been checked. It does no modelling and no estimation. It does not guess a date from a press release, a consultation, an announcement or a news report. If a country is not in the dataset, or its rules are announced but not yet law, the tool says so instead of showing dates.

Every date shown carries the instrument it comes from, so you can check it yourself.
